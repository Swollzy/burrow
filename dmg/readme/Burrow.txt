Burrow {{VERSION}}
==================

INSTALL (30 seconds)

1. Drag Burrow onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open your Applications folder and double-click Burrow.

THE FIRST TIME YOU OPEN IT

Burrow is free and isn't sold through the App Store, so the first time
macOS says it "can't check it for malicious software" (or "Apple could not
verify..."). That's normal for indie apps. To open it:

  1. Click Done (or OK) on that message.
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down. Next to "Burrow was blocked...", click Open Anyway.
  4. Enter your Mac password, then click Open Anyway again.

You only do this once. After that it opens like any other app.
(On macOS 14 Sonoma or older you can also right-click Burrow in
Applications, choose Open, then click Open.)

USING IT

- Start on the Setup tab. It downloads Wine (the free engine that runs
  Windows programs) and sets everything up. That's a few hundred MB.
- "Turbo graphics" builds a faster engine on your Mac. It needs Apple's
  free Command Line Tools; Burrow offers to install them.
- Then use the Steam tab to install Windows Steam and your games.

Burrow doesn't include Wine, DXMT or Steam; it downloads them from their
own projects. Games that need DirectX 12 or kernel anti-cheat won't run.

Needs macOS 13 Ventura or newer. Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
